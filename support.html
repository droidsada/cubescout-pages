Scanning with Flash

Keep the whole cube in the camera view with even lighting and your fingers clear of the tiles. Turn the whole cube horizontally first, then follow the instructions to show the remaining sides. Avoid turning individual layers during scanning.

If a scan doesn’t work

Open Settings in CubeScout and choose Email last scan log. You can describe what happened and review the email before sending. The attachment contains detection diagnostics, not camera images or audio. Only the latest stopped or completed scan log is retained on your device.

Playing in Freehand

Swipe a row or column to turn a layer. Twist with two fingers or drag outside the cube to rotate the view. Turn your phone sideways for layer buttons. Undo and redo let you revisit turns; Scramble starts a new challenge. Your progress is saved on your device.
