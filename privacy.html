Privacy Policy

This policy describes CubeScout version 1.0 and this support website. CubeScout is provided by Sadaruwan Hettiarachchi. Contact: sadaruwansoft@gmail.com.

Camera processing stays on your device

Flash uses your camera, with your permission, to detect cube tiles and movement and build a virtual cube. Camera frames are processed on your device. CubeScout does not upload those frames or include camera images or audio in scan logs. You can change camera permission in your device settings.

Information saved locally

CubeScout saves Freehand progress and move history on your device so you can continue later. It also keeps one scan log: the latest completed or stopped scan. A subsequent completed or stopped scan replaces the previous log.

Scan logs can contain a randomly generated scan identifier, scan dates and duration, device model, operating system and app information, screen dimensions, detected cube colors and orientations, and performance and error diagnostics. They help investigate detection problems. Logs are not automatically sent to the developer.

Removing the app and its data removes these local files from that installation. Device backups may retain app data according to your operating system and backup settings.

Optional emails and scan reports

If you choose Email last scan log, CubeScout prepares an email with the latest saved log attached. Send suggestions prepares an email without automatically attaching a log. You choose whether to send either email.

If you send an email, the developer receives your email address, any sender name, the message, and any attachments you include. This information is used to respond to you, investigate faults, and improve CubeScout’s detection and features. Please avoid including sensitive personal information that is not needed for your request.

Service providers and sharing

Email is delivered through your chosen email provider and received using Gmail. These providers process email under their own terms and privacy policies. CubeScout does not sell your personal information or use it for advertising. Information may be disclosed where required by law.

Retention and your choices

Support messages and voluntarily submitted logs are retained for as long as needed to address the request and investigate the reported issue, or meet legal obligations. You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent. You may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, obtain a copy of your data, or complain to your local data protection authority, depending on where you live.

The app’s local functions do not require an account. Sending feedback is optional and does not affect your ability to use CubeScout. We use information you voluntarily send to provide the support you request and for our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising scripts or cookies to these pages. GitHub may process visitor information, including IP addresses, for hosting and security. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Email and website hosting providers may process information outside your country.

Children

CubeScout does not require personal details to play. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information that should be removed, please contact us.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision. For privacy questions or requests, email sadaruwansoft@gmail.com.
